How I Approach an Analysis

I treat analysis as a decision-making process, not just a technical exercise.

01

Frame the question

Define the decision, research question, or business problem before selecting metrics or building visuals.

02

Evaluate the evidence

Examine data sources, definitions, comparability, limitations, and the assumptions behind each measure.

03

Build a reliable process

Clean, transform, validate, and structure the data so the analysis can be updated and reused.

04

Communicate the meaning

Use visuals and narrative to explain what the findings mean, where uncertainty remains, and why the result matters.

About me

Finance experience combined with analytical curiosity.

My background is in financial planning and analysis, accounting, reporting, and business decision support. Over time, I expanded that work through data modeling, visualization, automation, and independent economic research.

I am especially interested in questions that require more than producing a number. I want to understand how the measure was constructed, whether the comparison is valid, what assumptions are being made, and how the result should influence a decision.

This portfolio documents both the finished work and the reasoning behind it.
